# Big Swing Golf

Your phone is the golf club: pick a club, aim, hold SET and swing for real, and your bean golfer, dressed the way you like, drives the ball down a seaside resort range. Club speed sets the distance, the club face at impact decides between a pure straight shot, a draw or a big slice. On the putting green, rock it like a putter and read the break. 1–4 players take turns on their own phones, or with controllers, keyboard, mouse or touch.
