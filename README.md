# Seaside Swing

Your phone is the golf club: pick a club, aim, hold SET and swing for real, and your bean golfer, dressed the way you like, drives the ball down a seaside resort. Club speed sets the distance, the club face at impact decides between a pure straight shot, a draw or a big slice. Rehearse with a practice swing, read the wind, and play a three-hole course with sand to stay out of, a closest-to-the-pin contest, target greens and a putting green with break to read. 1–4 players take turns on their own phones, or with controllers, keyboard, mouse or touch.
